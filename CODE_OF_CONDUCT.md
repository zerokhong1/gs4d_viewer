# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive; discuss ideas and code, not people; welcome newcomers and their questions; no
harassment or discrimination of any kind, in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere else in the project.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub (user `zerokhong1`). Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that break these rules, and may
block people who break them repeatedly.
